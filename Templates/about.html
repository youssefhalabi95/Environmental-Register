About Environmental Register Generator

Environmental Register Generator is a simple web-based tool designed to help environmental professionals structure environmental aspect and impact assessments.

What can it do?

Users can enter project information, identify environmental aspects and impacts, evaluate severity and likelihood, automatically calculate risk scores, and export the completed register to Excel.

Free Version

The free version currently allows users to create a register containing up to five environmental aspects.

Intended Use

This tool is intended to assist with environmental assessment and documentation. Users should apply their organization's own environmental assessment methodology, legal requirements, and professional judgment when evaluating environmental aspects and impacts.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Information You Enter

Environmental Register Generator allows you to enter project information and environmental assessment data in order to create an environmental register.

How Your Data Is Used

The information you enter is used to generate and display your environmental register and to create the Excel file requested by you.

Accounts

The current version does not require you to create a user account.

Cookies and Sessions

The application uses a session to temporarily maintain your project and register information while you use the tool.

Data Sharing

The current version does not intentionally sell or share your environmental register information with third parties.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the operator of Environmental Register Generator.
